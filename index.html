Attribution setup before launch
Critical
1
Install the ChatGPT Ads pixel on confirmation and sign-up pages before any campaign goes live
2
Connect the Conversions API to pass server-side purchase and sign-up events
Launched May 5 alongside self-serve most advertisers haven't set this up yet. Getting this live on day one is the highest-value onboarding action.
3
Set attribution window to 7–14 days minimum across all campaigns
⚠ 60% of ChatGPT ad conversions happen outside the same-session click window a 7-14 day attribution window is required to see the real picture. Brands measuring same-session only will undervalue this channel and cut budget prematurely.
Regulatory considerations
Health advertising
FDA prescription drug rules
All prescription references require "prescribed by licensed physician" qualifier. No efficacy claims without clinical substantiation.
FTC disclosure
Sponsored label must be clearly visible. No misleading comparative claims against competitors by name.
Before/after claims
Prohibited. No transformation language, no specific outcome guarantees in ad copy.
OpenAI ads principles
Paid ads never influence what ChatGPT recommends organically. User conversations are not shared with advertisers — brands receive aggregated performance data only. Clients in sensitive health categories will ask about this directly.
Health and telehealth brands require extra review time before launch. Flag legal and compliance teams early in the onboarding process — plan for a 2 week review cycle before any campaign goes live.
