How to write context hints

Context hints describe the conversation, not the query. Unlike keywords, they guide the matching system to find the right moment in an ongoing dialogue. A user who once Googled "ED treatment" now types a full paragraph into ChatGPT about symptoms, concerns, and what they have already tried.

Too broad
"erectile dysfunction treatment"
"hair loss"
"weight loss medication"
Right approach
"Men researching discreet treatment options for ED without visiting a doctor in person"
"Men in their 30s and 40s noticing thinning hair and looking for clinically proven options"
"People asking about GLP-1 options, comparing semaglutide alternatives and online prescriptions"

Measurement and tracking
Set up before launch
The Ads Manager shows impressions, clicks, CTR, and CPC natively. To understand whether those clicks are driving real business outcomes: sign-ups, subscriptions, revenue. Measurement infrastructure needs to be in place before any campaign goes live.
1
Install the OAIQ pixel on conversion pages
Place the pixel on Hims's subscription confirmation and sign-up pages before launch. Fire subscription_created and trial_started events. The OAIQ pixel sets a first-party cookie that is not affected by browser restrictions, making it more reliable than third-party attribution. Accounts with the pixel installed before June 1 qualify for Conversions objective access starting June 5.
2
Connect the Conversions API for server-side tracking
The Conversions API sends the same events server-side, deduplicating against pixel data. Fire the same events from the server as a fallback for cases where the browser pixel may be blocked. Use OpenAI's shared key to ensure events are not double-counted. This is the setup pattern every mature ad platform has converged on. OpenAI is using the same approach.
3
Tag all destination URLs with UTM parameters
UTMs persist on click and flow into GA4, making ChatGPT ad traffic identifiable and comparable against Google and Meta. Create a custom channel group called Paid AI to separate ChatGPT ad traffic from organic ChatGPT citations.
utm_source=chatgpt&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=ed-treatment
Budget guidance
Minimum to learn
$100/day minimum. Below this, volume is too low to draw any conclusions within a reasonable timeframe.
Recommended test budget
$3,000-5,000/month for meaningful data. Enough to compare ad groups and context hints with statistical confidence.
Learning phase
Conversions objective needs 50 conversions/week to exit learning. If volume is too low, start with Clicks and switch once data accumulates.
⚠ Conversions objective launches June 5 for accounts with the pixel installed before June 1. Advertisers without measurement in place will not qualify for early access. Getting the OAIQ pixel and Conversions API live on day one is the single most important onboarding action.
Regulatory considerations
Health advertising
Health brands require legal and compliance review before any campaign goes live. Flag this at the onboarding call, plan for a two-week review cycle, not a same-week launch.
FDA prescription rules
All prescription references require "prescribed by a licensed physician" qualifier. No efficacy claims without clinical substantiation.
FTC disclosure
Sponsored label must be clearly visible. No misleading comparative claims against named competitors.
Before and after claims
Prohibited. No transformation language or specific outcome guarantees in ad copy.
